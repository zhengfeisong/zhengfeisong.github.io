🤖 AeroEye: Snake-like Robot System for Aircraft Engine Damage Detection

#object detection#robotic perception#deep learning
Project details

AeroEye is a snake-like robot system designed to inspect aircraft engines for damage. Its flexible structure enables access to confined spaces, combining robotic mobility with visual defect detection.

I joined Tongji University’s QiDi program and served as a key team member under the guidance of Prof. Peng Qi. Supported by the China National University Student Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Program, I was primarily responsible for robotic perception and contributed to system development.

Patents

I am the first student inventor on two invention patent applications, with my advisor listed as the first inventor: a YOLOv5-based method for aircraft engine crack detection, and an obstacle avoidance method and system for an aircraft engine defect detection robot. Both applications are currently undergoing substantive examination.
