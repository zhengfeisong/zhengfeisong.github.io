Zhengfei Song

Hi there! I’m Zhengfei Song, a first-year master’s student in Electrical Engineering at the California Institute of Technology (Caltech), within the Division of Engineering and Applied Science. I am honored to be supported by the Chiang Chen Overseas Fellowship ($50,000).

I received my Bachelor of Engineering in Automation from the College of Electronics and Information Engineering at Tongji University in July 2026. During my undergraduate studies, I conducted research in semantic segmentation with the Machine Intelligence & Autonomous Systems (MIAS) Group under the supervision of Prof. Rui Fan.

My research interests lie in intelligent systems, with a focus on perception, multimodal learning, and real-world applications of machine learning. My goal is to develop systems that integrate diverse sources of information to perceive and understand the world, enabling reliable and adaptive behavior in complex environments.
